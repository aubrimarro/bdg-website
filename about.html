About

Read More About Our Story!

Big Daddy Graphics began in the summer of 1994 in the basement of Mindy and Rex Russell’s home. At the time, both were working as police officers while building their screen-printing business on the side. What started as a small basement operation continued to grow, eventually leading them to purchase the shop on Burlingwood Drive and eventually added a separate production facility off Eckel Road in Perrysburg.

Today, Big Daddy Graphics remains a family-run business. Mindy runs the company, while their son Zeb oversees production at the Eckel Road facility. Willow helps run the retail store and builds online stores for schools, teams and other groups. Maddie, Rex and Mindy’s daughter, manages the company’s social media from San Diego. Karen and Rex create the designs.

Creativity has always been an important part of the business. Rex, an artist, served as Big Daddy’s primary designer for many years. Although he has stepped back from the day-to-day business to focus on painting and participating in local art shows, he still contributes design work alongside Karen.

From a basement print shop to a retail store and full production operation, Big Daddy Graphics has grown considerably while maintaining the personal, family-run approach on which it was founded.
